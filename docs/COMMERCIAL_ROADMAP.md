# Commercial Roadmap

## Positioning

The strongest commercial positioning is not a generic interview chatbot. It should be positioned as:

```text
An AI career coach for students and junior developers preparing for AI application development roles.
```

## Best Customer Segments

1. University AI clubs and CS departments
2. Coding bootcamps and training programs
3. Career coaches who serve technical candidates
4. Small teams hiring AI interns or junior AI engineers

## Why B2B / Education First

Individual candidates may pay once and leave. Schools and training programs have repeated cohorts, multiple users, and a clear need for dashboards and progress tracking.

## Commercial Features

### Phase 1: Portfolio Demo

- Public landing/demo page
- Stable interview flow
- GitHub README, CI, architecture docs
- 2-minute demo video

### Phase 2: School / Bootcamp Pilot

- Organization accounts
- Class dashboard
- Candidate progress table
- Weakness distribution by cohort
- Exportable PDF reports
- Custom question bank upload

### Phase 3: Paid Product

- Monthly organization plan
- Per-seat candidate access
- Admin question bank management
- Prompt/version analytics
- Retrieval quality dashboard
- Model cost and latency dashboard

## Pricing Experiment

Start with small pilots:

- Free: public demo and one interview
- Student Pro: low-cost monthly plan for repeated practice
- Organization Pilot: fixed monthly fee for a cohort

Do not optimize pricing before validating that users actually finish interviews and share reports.

## Competition Angle

For hackathons or competitions, pitch it as:

```text
Agentic AI Career Coach for AI Application Developer Candidates
```

Judges usually care about:

- Real user pain
- Working demo
- Technical depth
- Measurable evaluation
- Deployment and reliability
- Clear next business step
